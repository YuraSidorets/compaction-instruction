Keep the full working state of the session in the summary, in its own section. Write one line for each item of the state, in the form "<name>: <current value>". An item is a decision, a value, a name, a path, a number, a constraint, a question or a piece of work. Where a value or a decision replaced an earlier one, add a line directly below it in the form "<name> history: <first value>, then <next value>", with every earlier value in order. For a decision, put its reason on its line, and put its earlier reasons on its history line. For a constraint that the user set, state whether it still applies. For a question, state open or answered, and the answer. For a piece of work, state done, open or blocked, and what blocks it. Copy names, paths and numbers character for character. Do not leave out an item because it did not change, do not merge items, and do not replace exact values with a description.
